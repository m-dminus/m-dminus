01 / Workflow

From intraoral scan to finished appliance.

One digital thread runs through every case, from the scan your practice takes to the appliance delivered back for the delivery appointment.

  1. The practice takes an intraoral scan of the patient and sends the case. The scan is the case.

  2. The appliance is digitally designed on that scan, for the individual case.

  3. Precision-fabricated from the design. Digitally exact.

  4. Hand-finished. Every case is inspected before it leaves the lab.

  5. The finished appliance is delivered back to the practice for the scheduled delivery appointment.
